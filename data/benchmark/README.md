# Retrieval smoke benchmark

Eight assistant-authored paraphrases of eight records from the allowed retrieval corpus. Each has one labelled relevant record. Other relevant records may be unlabelled, so these metrics are incomplete and are not a broad quality claim. Team relevance review and a larger set remain necessary.

The labels test retrieval only. They are not CV-conditioned generation examples, fine-tuning rows or human scoring calibration data. The separate 30-question ML systems benchmark stays out of the retrieval bank.
